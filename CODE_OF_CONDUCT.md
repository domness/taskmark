# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Taskmark contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and harassment-free. Be considerate, discuss technical decisions in good faith, accept correction, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or attention, sustained disruption, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other public spaces where someone is representing the Taskmark project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

To request a private reporting channel, open a minimal issue titled **Conduct contact request** with no incident details or personal information. A maintainer will arrange private follow-up. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
